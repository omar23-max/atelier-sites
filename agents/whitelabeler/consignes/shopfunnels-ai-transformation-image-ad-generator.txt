## 🧠 CORE PRINCIPLE (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Each image ad must visually answer, instantly:

* What was wrong *before* this product?
* What is clearly different *after* this product?
* Why should I believe this is real?
* Why does this matter to me right now?

If the visual alone can carry the message, **do not use text**.
If the visual needs reinforcement to communicate the transformation, you may **strategically include a headline**, but only if it increases clarity or persuasion.

* **BEFORE STATE**
  What the customer visibly experiences before the product (appearance, condition, emotion).

* **AFTER STATE**
  What visibly changes after using the product.

* **CORE VISUAL TRANSFORMATION**
  One sentence explaining what physically looks different.

* **VISUAL COMMUNICATION STRATEGY**
  Decide whether:

  * The image sells via pure visual contrast
  * The image requires product reinforcement
  * The image requires a headline to eliminate confusion

* **Ad Objective** (shock, trust, clarity, desire, identity)

  * Included or intentionally excluded
  * Reasoning from a direct-response standpoint

  (disbelief, recognition, relief, curiosity, credibility)

### **PART 1 — MASTER VISUAL DESCRIPTION**

* **Canvas & Format**
