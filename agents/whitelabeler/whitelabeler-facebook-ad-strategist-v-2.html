AI Employee · Facebook Ad Strategist

Turn a competitor's winning Facebook ads into your own launch plan.

Tell it your product and a brand you want to model. It pulls that brand's entire ad library out of Winning Hunter, works out which ads are actually profitable and why, then writes you a full ad strategy and builds the Excel workbook you launch from.

What this does

Most people launching Facebook ads are guessing at campaign structure, budgets, and creative. This AI Employee removes the guessing by copying the homework of a brand that has already made it work. It reads a competitor's live ad library, identifies the ads that have been running longest (longevity means they are profitable, or the brand would have switched them off), explains why each one works, then converts all of it into a plan you can execute this week.

Use it when
  • You are about to launch Facebook ads and do not know how to structure the account
  • There is a competitor whose ads you want to reverse engineer
  • You want a testing plan with real kill rules instead of guessing when to switch an ad off
  • You have no brand in mind and want it to find the best one in your niche to model
What you get
  • A full strategy report in the chat: campaign structure, targeting, creative specs, and the gaps you can attack
  • An eight tab Excel workbook you download
  • Verified links to the exact ads to model, each checked live so none of them are dead
  • Ready to paste Meta ad copy, ABO and CBO testing plans, scaling rules, and a budget calculator
What you need
  • A Winning Hunter account, Basic plan or above, since the ad data comes from there
  • Winning Hunter connected to your AI tool as a connector. It walks you through the setup before anything else runs
  • Your product, and ideally your selling price and product cost, which power the budget calculator

How it works

No ad ships unverified

Every candidate's Ad Library link gets fetched before it reaches the workbook. Expired ones are dropped and replaced, and if the count comes up short it tells you plainly instead of padding the tab.

Every threshold is a formula

Breakeven ROAS, max profitable CPA, per ad set budgets, and the kill and keep rules all read from the budget tab. Nothing is hardcoded, so changing your price rewrites the plan.

Estimates stay labelled

Spend, reach, and revenue come back as estimates and are marked as such in the chat and in the workbook. It never promises performance, and it tells you to adapt the ads rather than copy them.
