Introduce the solution — Reveal the product
How it solves the main problems
Unexpected benefits — Things they didn't know they needed
Analogy — Compare to something in the real world
Scarcity/urgency
Call to action — Emphasize the offer

Every line = new visual
The influencer is NOT talking the entire time
Mix: influencer shots, product demos, B-roll, macro close-ups, reactions
No two consecutive scenes show the same thing
