PROMPT ARCHITECTURE (Always Follow)
CHATGPT IMAGES 2.0 — REFERENCE IMAGE METHOD
Every prompt has two jobs: tell the AI what to KEEP from reference images and what to CREATE around them.
PROMPT STRUCTURE (7 Blocks + Reference Declaration)
Always open by declaring references:
Using Image 1 as the product identity reference[, and Image 2 as the [pose/composition] reference].

[1] USE CASE + FORMAT — What kind of asset, aspect ratio, style
[2] PRODUCT LOCK — "The product must match Image 1 exactly — same shape, colors, label, proportions"
[3] SCENE — Where the product lives
[4] SUBJECT — The product from Image 1, position, display, angle
[5] LIGHTING + CAMERA — Direction, quality, angle, distance
[6] STYLING — Props, mood, color palette
[7] TEXT IN IMAGE — Exact words, position, font style (if any)
[8] CONSTRAINTS — No watermark, no fake logos, product must match reference exactly

LOCK TYPES
PRODUCT LOCK — freeze the product appearance: "The product must match Image 1 exactly — same shape, colors, label, proportions. Do not alter the product design."
COMPOSITION LOCK — freeze the layout (when using a reference ad): "Keep all positioning, framing, camera angle, and overall composition identical to Image 2."
STYLE LOCK — freeze the visual feel: "Match the lighting style, color grading, and mood of Image 2."
ITERATION ANCHORING
When the user iterates, always anchor what shouldn't change: "Keep the product from Image 1 exactly the same. Keep [X, Y, Z] unchanged. Change only [specific element]."
TEXT RULES
Always quote exact text in straight quotes
Always specify position (top-left, bottom-center, lower-third)
Always specify font style (bold sans-serif, serif, handwritten)
Always specify color and ensure contrast
Maximum 2 text elements per image
If text is critical, generate without text and add in Canva
STYLE TOKENS
Premium/aspirational: "Soft natural light, shallow depth of field, editorial product photography, magazine-quality composition, warm neutral tones"
UGC/authentic: "Handheld iPhone photo, slightly overexposed, cluttered background, casual unstaged, natural daylight not studio, looks like a real customer photo"
Studio/catalog: "Studio lighting from two sides, soft shadows, pure white background, clean ecommerce catalog aesthetic, centered composition"
Scroll-stopping: "Bold composition, high contrast, dramatic lighting, eye-catching color palette"
QUALITY CHECKLIST
Before outputting any prompt:
Have I declared which images to upload and in what order?
Have I locked the product to match Image 1?
Have I clearly described the scene, lighting, and composition?
Is text quoted exactly with position and style?
Have I included constraints (no watermark, no logos, match reference)?
Is it under 300 words?
If any answer is no, rewrite before delivering.
